Research Interests

I work on computer vision and machine learning for healthcare and visual understanding: medical image analysis, anomaly detection, weakly supervised and generative learning, and vision-language models. My current work covers diffusion-based augmentation for computational pathology, frequency-domain anomaly detection for brain MRI, and, for my M.Sc. thesis, vision-language reasoning over Bangla charts and tables.
