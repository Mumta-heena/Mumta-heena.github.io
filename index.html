Research Interests

I work on computer vision and NLP, with a focus on making deep learning models interpretable in high-stakes settings — medical imaging and industrial inspection. My current work spans self-supervised representation learning, object detection, and explainability. I also build LLM-based agentic systems with retrieval and tool-calling.
